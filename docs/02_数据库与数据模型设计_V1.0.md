# 数据库与数据模型设计 V1.0

## 命名
- MySQL 列：snake_case
- Java/JSON：lowerCamelCase
- 第一版统一 Asia/Shanghai 业务时区

## 核心表
1. `users(id, username, password_hash, name, role, status, created_at, updated_at)`
2. `courses(id, course_name, teacher_id, description, created_at, updated_at)`
3. `course_students(id, course_id, student_id, joined_at, status)`，唯一 `(course_id, student_id)`
4. `exams(id, course_id, title, description, start_time, end_time, late_entry_minutes, min_submit_minutes, status, created_by, published_at, created_at, updated_at)`
5. `questions(id, exam_id, question_type, content, score, options_json, reference_answer, sort_order, created_at, updated_at)`
6. `scoring_points(id, question_id, point_content, point_score, sort_order)`
7. `submissions(id, exam_id, student_id, first_enter_time, last_enter_time, submit_time, status, total_score, teacher_comment, score_published, published_at, created_at, updated_at)`，唯一 `(exam_id, student_id)`
8. `answers(id, submission_id, question_id, answer_content, auto_score, teacher_score, teacher_comment, grading_status, created_at, updated_at)`，唯一 `(submission_id, question_id)`
9. `ai_grading_results(id, answer_id, ai_score, ai_reason, ai_comment, matched_points, missing_points, status, error_message, created_at, updated_at)`，`answer_id` 唯一

## 固定枚举
- `users.role`: `TEACHER | STUDENT`
- `exams.status`: `DRAFT | PUBLISHED | GRADING | RELEASED`
- `questions.question_type`: `SINGLE_CHOICE | SHORT_ANSWER`
- `submissions.status`: `IN_PROGRESS | SUBMITTED | AUTO_SUBMITTED | GRADED`
- `answers.grading_status`: `PENDING | AUTO_GRADED | AI_GRADED | REVIEWED`

## 关键规则
- 首次成功进入考试时创建 submission 并写 first_enter_time。
- 超过 start_time + late_entry_minutes 且没有首次进入记录 → 拒考。
- 选择题自动分写 auto_score；简答题 AI 仅写 ai_grading_results，教师最终分写 teacher_score。
- total_score 必须由后端计算，前端不能直接传最终总分。
- 学生成绩只有 score_published=1 后可读。
